	Econ scan scene.

	IDs can come from the barcode scanner, from typing in the box
	and pressing Next, or from the camera window ("Econ Reader"):
	hold the ECON still under the camera until the rebuilt code
	shows "OK", then click "Send to GUI" (or press Enter in that
	window). A sent ID is handled just like a scanner read.
